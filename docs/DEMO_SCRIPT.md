1. Open http://localhost:3000 — show privacy panel reports **Local processing**.
2. Drop `data/images/I01_person_car.png` — narration starts; highlight follows segments.
3. Ask “What color is the shirt?” — play the spoken answer.
4. Switch to Webpage mode — paste `fixture:simple_article.html` (or a public article URL).
5. Show section-by-section highlight while audio plays.
6. Toggle Blur / Reduced Color preview; read the non-clinical disclaimer.
7. State the quality trade-off vs large cloud multimodal models honestly.
